The startup splash is part of the plugin, not of the Furina theme. The theme only
adds a separate `<style id="furina-startup-skin">` block that redeclares the
plugin's `--dsu-*` variables per skin and paints a wallpaper plate behind its
content. Removing this folder does not affect the theme.
